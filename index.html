Can Frontier Agents Invent Robot Tools?

Matei Gardea

Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences

Abstract

We study whether Opus 5.5, Astra 6, and Sol 6.1 can design tools for five manipulation tasks using a simulated Franka Panda. Each model proposes a tool and a sequence of robot movements, tests them in simulation, and revises its proposal over four attempts. Averaged across the five tasks, success rates are 90.7% for Astra 6, 86.7% for Sol 6.1, 73.3% for Opus 5.5.

Results

Each model produces three independent designs per task. Each selected design is tested in ten scenes with small position changes.
